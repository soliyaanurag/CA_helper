# How to file GSTR-3B yourself

<!-- Reviewed on 29 Sep 2026 against the official pages listed at the bottom (V1). Content rule
     (content/README.md): no legal thresholds, rates or due dates here; the app shows them from the
     rule tables. -->

## Before you start
Tick the documents in the checklist on this page, and keep your login details ready. File this period's GSTR-1
first.

## Steps
1. Log in to the GST portal and open **Services → Returns → Returns Dashboard**.
2. Choose the financial year and the period, then open **GSTR-3B → Prepare online**.
3. Check the sales figures, which are filled in from your GSTR-1.
4. Check the input tax credit against **GSTR-2B** (the statement of credit from your suppliers).
5. Save, then **Proceed to payment**: pay any tax due from the electronic cash or credit ledger. Interest for late payment is worked out by the portal.
6. **File** with DSC or EVC (OTP) and download the acknowledgement with the **ARN**.

If you had no sales, no purchases and nothing to pay, a **nil GSTR-3B** may be possible (online, or by SMS): see the official FAQ on nil filing.

## After filing
Click **Mark as filed** on this page and upload the acknowledgement (PDF or photo), so you and your CA can
always find it. The app reads the acknowledgement on our server and marks the filing "Filed–verified" when it
matches.

## Common mistakes
- Claiming credit that is not in GSTR-2B.
- Filing without paying the tax due.
- Sales figures that do not match GSTR-1.
