# GSTR-3B: what it is and why it matters

<!-- Reviewed on 29 Sep 2026 against the official pages listed at the bottom (V1). Content rule

## What is it?
GSTR-3B is the summary GST return in which you **pay your GST**. You report your total sales, the tax credit you claim on purchases (input tax credit), and pay the difference. Much of it is filled in for you from your GSTR-1 and from GSTR-2B (the statement of credit from your suppliers).

## Who has to file it?
Businesses registered under the regular GST scheme, even for a period with no business at all (then as a nil return). The app shows it when your profile says "Regular" GST. Composition-scheme businesses file CMP-08 instead.

## How often, and for which period?
Monthly, or quarterly if you are in the QRMP scheme. In QRMP you still pay tax for the first two months of each quarter with a separate challan. The due date is shown on this page and in your calendar; under QRMP it depends on the state of your principal place of business.

## What happens if it is late or skipped?
Late filing brings a late fee, and tax paid late brings interest. A GSTR-3B cannot be filed before the GSTR-1 of the same period, and later returns wait for the missing ones.

## Filing it yourself vs. with a CA
Filing it yourself is reasonable when your sales and purchases are simple and your GSTR-1 is filed. A CA helps when the tax credit in GSTR-2B does not match your purchase records.
