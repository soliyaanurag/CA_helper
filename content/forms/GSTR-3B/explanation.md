# GSTR-3B: what it is and why it matters

## What is it?
GSTR-3B is the summary GST return in which you **pay your GST**. You report your total sales, the tax credit you claim on purchases (input tax credit), and pay the difference.

## Who has to file it?
Businesses registered under the regular GST scheme. The app shows it when your profile says "Regular" GST. Composition-scheme businesses file CMP-08 instead.

## How often, and for which period?
Monthly, or quarterly if you are in the QRMP scheme. In QRMP you still pay tax for the first two months of each quarter with a separate challan. The due date is shown on this page and in your calendar.

## What happens if it is late or skipped?
Late filing brings a late fee for each day, and tax paid late brings interest. While a GSTR-3B is pending, the next one cannot be filed.

## Filing it yourself vs. with a CA
Filing it yourself is reasonable when your sales and purchases are simple and your GSTR-1 is filed. A CA helps when the tax credit in GSTR-2B does not match your purchase records.
