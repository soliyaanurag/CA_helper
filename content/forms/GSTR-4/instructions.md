# How to file GSTR-4 yourself

## Before you start
Tick the documents in the checklist on this page, and keep your login details ready.

## Steps
1. Log in to the GST portal and open **Services → Returns → Returns Dashboard**.
2. Choose the financial year and open **GSTR-4 (Annual return)**.
3. Check the turnover and tax, filled in from your CMP-08 statements.
4. Add the year's purchases (inward supplies), from registered and unregistered suppliers.
5. Preview, then **File** with DSC or EVC (OTP) and download the acknowledgement with the **ARN**.

## After filing
Click **Mark as filed** on this page and upload the acknowledgement (PDF or photo), so you and your CA can
always find it.

## Common mistakes
- Filing before every CMP-08 of the year is filed.
- Leaving out purchases from unregistered suppliers.
