# CMP-08: what it is and why it matters

## What is it?
CMP-08 is the statement in which a business in the **composition scheme** reports its turnover and pays its GST for the quarter. It replaces GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for these businesses.

## Who has to file it?
Businesses that chose the GST composition scheme. The app shows it when your profile says "Composition".

## How often, and for which period?
Quarterly, one statement per quarter of the financial year. The due date is shown on this page and in your calendar.

## What happens if it is late or skipped?
A late statement brings a late fee, and tax paid late brings interest. Repeated delays can cause trouble with your registration.

## Filing it yourself vs. with a CA
Most composition businesses can file it themselves: it needs only the quarter's turnover. A CA helps if you also have purchases under reverse charge or are unsure whether you still qualify.
