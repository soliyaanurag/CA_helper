# How to file Income tax return (ITR) yourself

<!-- Reviewed on 29 Sep 2026 against the official pages listed at the bottom (V1). Content rule
     (content/README.md): no legal thresholds, rates or due dates here; the app shows them from the

## Before you start
Tick the documents in the checklist on this page, and keep your login details ready.

## Steps
1. Log in to the income-tax e-filing portal with your PAN.
2. Open **e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return**, and choose the year: the assessment year for income up to March 2026 (for example AY 2026-27), or the tax year from April 2026 (for example tax year 2026-27).
3. Choose the ITR form your profile shows, and check the figures filled in for you against your **AIS** and **Form 26AS**.
4. Enter your business income, deductions and any tax already paid (TDS, advance tax).
5. Pay any tax still due through **e-Pay Tax**, then submit the return.
6. **E-verify** it (Aadhaar OTP, net banking, a bank account or a digital signature) and download the acknowledgement (ITR-V).

## After filing
Click **Mark as filed** on this page and upload the acknowledgement (PDF or photo), so you and your CA can
always find it. The app reads the acknowledgement on our server and marks the filing "Filed–verified" when it
matches.

## Common mistakes
- Income that does not match AIS / Form 26AS.
- Submitting but never e-verifying the return.
- Using the wrong ITR form.
