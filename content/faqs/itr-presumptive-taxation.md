# Presumptive taxation: sections 44AD and 44ADA

## What is it?
A simplified way for small businesses and professionals to pay income tax. Instead of keeping detailed books and working out actual profit, you **declare a fixed percentage of your turnover or receipts as income**.

## For businesses: section 44AD
- **Who:** resident individuals, HUFs and **partnership firms (not LLPs)** running an eligible business.
- **Turnover limit:** up to **₹2 crore**, or up to **₹3 crore if cash receipts are not more than 5%** of total receipts.
- **Income to declare:** at least **8% of turnover**, or **6%** of the part received through banking or digital modes (account-payee cheque, bank transfer, UPI and similar) by the return due date.
- **Not for:** professionals under 44ADA, commission agents and brokers, or goods-carriage businesses (those use 44AE).

## For professionals: section 44ADA
- **Who:** resident individuals and partnership firms (not LLPs) in specified professions, such as legal, medical, engineering, architecture, accountancy, technical consultancy, interior decoration, and other notified professions.
- **Receipts limit:** up to **₹50 lakh**, or up to **₹75 lakh if cash receipts are not more than 5%**.
- **Income to declare:** at least **50% of gross receipts**.

## Benefits
- No need to maintain detailed books of account for that income.
- No tax audit, as long as you declare at least the presumptive income.
- **Advance tax in one instalment** by 15 March instead of four.

## The 5-year rule (44AD)
If you opt for 44AD and then, within the next five years, declare profit below the presumptive rate, you **cannot use 44AD for the following five years**. You may then also need a tax audit if your income is above the basic exemption limit.

## Which ITR form?
**ITR-4** if total income is up to ₹50 lakh and you meet its other conditions; otherwise **ITR-3** (or ITR-5 for firms).

## Under the new Income-tax Act, 2025
From tax year 2026-27, sections 44AD, 44ADA and 44AE are combined into **section 58**. The rates and limits above are carried forward.
