# GST late fees and interest for late returns

## Late fee for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B
The late fee runs **for every day after the due date** until you file:
- **₹50 per day** (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST) when the return has tax or transactions
- **₹20 per day** (₹10 CGST + ₹10 SGST) for a **nil** return

## The late fee is capped
Since June 2021 the total late fee per return is capped, based on your **aggregate turnover in the previous financial year**:

| Case | Maximum late fee per return (CGST + SGST) |
|---|---|
| Nil return | ₹500 |
| Turnover up to ₹1.5 crore | ₹2,000 |
| Turnover above ₹1.5 crore and up to ₹5 crore | ₹5,000 |
| Turnover above ₹5 crore | ₹10,000 |

**Example:** a business with ₹80 lakh turnover files GSTR-3B 30 days late. The fee is 30 × ₹50 = ₹1,500, which is below the ₹2,000 cap, so it pays ₹1,500. If it were 60 days late, the fee would be 60 × ₹50 = ₹3,000, but the cap limits it to ₹2,000.

The GST portal calculates the GSTR-3B late fee automatically. For GSTR-1 the fee applies under the law even if the portal does not add it at the time of filing.

## Interest on tax paid late
If tax is paid after the due date, **interest at 18% a year** is charged on the tax paid late, for the days of delay. It applies to the part of the tax paid in cash, not to tax set off using input tax credit.

## Composition taxpayers
- **GSTR-4** (annual return): ₹50 per day, capped at ₹2,000, or ₹500 for a nil return.
- **CMP-08** (quarterly payment): interest at 18% a year on tax paid late. Check the GST portal for whether a late fee is currently levied.
