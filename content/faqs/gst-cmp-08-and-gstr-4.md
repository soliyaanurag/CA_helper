# CMP-08 and GSTR-4: returns for composition taxpayers

## CMP-08: quarterly statement and payment
**CMP-08** is how a composition taxpayer declares turnover and **pays tax every quarter**.

| Quarter | Due date |
|---|---|
| April-June | 18 July |
| July-September | 18 October |
| October-December | 18 January |
| January-March | 18 April |

File it even when turnover is nil. If you pay late, **interest at 18% a year** applies to the tax paid late.

## GSTR-4: annual return
**GSTR-4** is the yearly return of a composition taxpayer. It summarises the year's turnover, tax paid through CMP-08 and purchases.

- **Due date: 30 June after the end of the financial year.** This applies from FY 2024-25 onwards; it was 30 April earlier. So GSTR-4 for FY 2025-26 was due on 30 June 2026, and for FY 2026-27 it is due on 30 June 2027.
- **Late fee:** ₹50 per day, capped at ₹2,000, or ₹500 if there was no tax liability.

## Common questions
**I stopped business activity. Do I still file?** Yes. Nil CMP-08s and a GSTR-4 are due until your registration is cancelled.

**Can I revise a filed GSTR-4 or CMP-08?** Filed returns generally cannot be revised; corrections are made in the next period's return where allowed. Ask a CA if you find an error.
