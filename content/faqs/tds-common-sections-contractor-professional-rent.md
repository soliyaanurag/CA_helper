# Common TDS cases for small businesses: contractors, professional fees, rent

The rates and limits below apply from FY 2025-26. Under the new Income-tax Act, 2025 (from 1 April 2026), all of these moved into one table in **section 393**. The rates and limits were largely carried over, but challans and statements use the new section references and payment codes.

## Payments to contractors (old section 194C)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | **1%** if the contractor is an individual or HUF; **2%** for others (firms, companies...) |
| **When** | A single payment above **₹30,000**, or total payments to that contractor above **₹1,00,000** in the year |
| **Examples** | Job work, repairs and maintenance contracts, transport contracts, catering, advertising production |

## Professional and technical fees (old section 194J)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | **10%** for professional services (CA, lawyer, doctor, architect, consultant...); **2%** for technical services (and call-centre services and some royalties) |
| **When** | Total payments above **₹50,000 in the year** for each category. The limit was raised from ₹30,000 from FY 2025-26. |
| **Note** | Fees, commission or other payments (other than salary) to a company **director** have **no threshold** |

## Rent (old section 194-I)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Rate** | **10%** for land, building or furniture; **2%** for plant and machinery |
| **When** | Rent above **₹50,000 per month** (or part of a month). This replaced the earlier ₹2,40,000 annual limit from FY 2025-26. |

## If the payee has no PAN
TDS is generally deducted at **20%** (or the normal rate, if higher) when the payee doesn't provide a valid PAN.

## Common mistakes
- Applying 10% to technical services, which usually attract 2%.
- Forgetting the annual total. Many small payments to one contractor can cross the ₹1,00,000 limit.
- Deducting but depositing late, which attracts interest (see *tds-who-must-deduct-and-tan.md*).

Before deducting, check the current rate for your exact case on the Income Tax Department website or with a CA.
