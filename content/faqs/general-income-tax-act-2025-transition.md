# The new Income-tax Act, 2025: what changed from 1 April 2026

## What happened?
India replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 with the **Income-tax Act, 2025**. The new Act came into force on **1 April 2026**. It keeps most tax rules the same but renumbers the sections, simplifies the wording and renames many forms.

## "Tax year" replaces "previous year" and "assessment year"
The old law used two terms: the *previous year* (when you earn income) and the *assessment year* (the next year, when you file). The new Act uses one term, the **tax year**, which runs from 1 April to 31 March.

## Which law applies to my return?
- **Income earned in FY 2025-26** (1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026): the return is for **Assessment Year 2026-27** and is filed under the **old 1961 Act**, using the old ITR forms (ITR-1 to ITR-7), in 2026.
- **Income earned from 1 April 2026** (Tax Year 2026-27 onwards): governed by the **new 2025 Act**. Those returns are filed in 2027 onwards.

AY 2026-27 and Tax Year 2026-27 are two different things: the first is about income of FY 2025-26, the second about income of FY 2026-27.

## Common section and form renumbering (old → new)
| Topic | Old (1961 Act) | New (2025 Act) |
|---|---|---|
| Filing an income tax return | Section 139 | Section 263 |
| Tax audit | Section 44AB | Section 63 |
| Presumptive taxation (business, professionals, goods carriages) | Sections 44AD, 44ADA, 44AE | Section 58 |
| TDS on salary | Section 192 | Section 392 |
| TDS on other payments (contractors, professional fees, rent...) | Sections 193-194T | Section 393 |
| TCS | Section 206C | Section 394 |
| Quarterly TDS statement - salary | Form 24Q | Form 138 |
| Quarterly TDS statement - non-salary payments to residents | Form 26Q | Form 140 |
| Salary TDS certificate | Form 16 | Form 130 |

For most small businesses the **rates, turnover limits and due dates stayed the same**. What changed is the section numbers you see on forms and challans.

## Periods before 1 April 2026
Everything relating to periods before 1 April 2026 (returns, TDS statements for January-March 2026, corrections, assessments) continues under the old Act and old form numbers, even if you file it after April 2026.

## Note for CA Helper users
Some screens still use the familiar names (24Q, 26Q, section 44AD). For tax year 2026-27 onwards, these correspond to the new forms and sections in the table above.
