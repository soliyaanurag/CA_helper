# TDS returns: due dates, late fee and certificates

## What is a TDS return?
Every quarter, a deductor files a **TDS statement** listing each deduction: who was paid, how much, how much tax was deducted, and the challan it was deposited with. This is how the payee's tax credit shows up in their Form 26AS / AIS.

## Which form? (the forms were renamed in 2026)
| What it covers | Up to FY 2025-26 (payments until 31 March 2026) | From tax year 2026-27 (payments from 1 April 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| TDS on **salary** | **Form 24Q** | **Form 138** |
| TDS on **other payments to residents** (contractors, professional fees, rent, commission, interest...) | **Form 26Q** | **Form 140** |
| TDS on payments to non-residents | Form 27Q | Form 144 |
| TCS (tax collected at source) | Form 27EQ | Form 143 |

A quarter that belongs to the old law (e.g. January-March 2026) stays on the old form, including corrections filed later.

## Due dates (every year)
| Quarter | Return due by |
|---|---|
| April-June | 31 July |
| July-September | 31 October |
| October-December | 31 January |
| January-March | 31 May |

## Filing late
- **Late fee: ₹200 per day** until you file, capped at the total TDS in that statement (section 234E of the old Act; the new Act has an equivalent provision).
- A separate **penalty** of ₹10,000 to ₹1,00,000 can be imposed in some cases, e.g. when the statement is not filed within a year of the due date.
- The deductees don't get credit for their tax until you file, which often causes disputes with them.

## TDS certificates
- **Salary:** an annual certificate to each employee by **15 June** after the year ends (Form 16 under the old law; **Form 130** under the new law).
- **Other payments:** a quarterly certificate generated from TRACES after you file the quarterly statement (Form 16A under the old law). Check the new form number and timeline for tax year 2026-27.

## In CA Helper
The calendar shows these statements under their familiar names (24Q / 26Q). For periods from April 2026, file them as **Form 138 / Form 140**.
