# Which income tax return (ITR) form should I file?

The right form depends on **who you are** (an individual, a firm, a company...) and **what kind of income** you have. For income of FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), the forms are ITR-1 to ITR-7 under the old Income-tax Act, 1961.

| Form | Who uses it (in short) |
|---|---|
| **ITR-1 (Sahaj)** | Resident individuals with total income up to ₹50 lakh from salary or pension, house property, and other sources such as interest. **No business or professional income.** |
| **ITR-2** | Individuals and HUFs **without business or professional income** who can't use ITR-1, e.g. capital gains above the ITR-1 limit or foreign assets. |
| **ITR-3** | Individuals and HUFs **with business or professional income** who keep regular accounts (not using the presumptive scheme, or not eligible for ITR-4). |
| **ITR-4 (Sugam)** | Resident individuals, HUFs and **partnership firms (not LLPs)** with total income up to ₹50 lakh whose business or professional income is computed under the **presumptive scheme** (44AD, 44ADA or 44AE). |
| **ITR-5** | **Firms, LLPs**, AOPs, BOIs and similar entities (not individuals, HUFs or companies). |
| **ITR-6** | **Companies** (other than those claiming exemption as charitable institutions). |
| **ITR-7** | Trusts, political parties, institutions and others filing under specific exemption sections. |

## Quick guide for small businesses
- **Freelancer or small trader using the presumptive scheme, income up to ₹50 lakh:** usually **ITR-4**.
- **Proprietor keeping full books** (or turnover above the presumptive limits): **ITR-3**.
- **Partnership firm:** **ITR-4** if eligible for the presumptive scheme, otherwise **ITR-5**.
- **LLP:** always **ITR-5**; LLPs cannot use the presumptive scheme.
- **Private limited company:** **ITR-6**.

## Things to keep in mind
- Each year's forms can change a little. The form instructions on the e-filing portal list the exact conditions.
- Using the wrong form can make the return **defective**, and the department may ask you to file again.
- From tax year 2026-27 (income from 1 April 2026), returns are filed under the new Income-tax Act, 2025 (see *general-income-tax-act-2025-transition.md*). Check the portal for the forms notified for that year.
