# Tax audit: when is it required?

## What is a tax audit?
A check of your accounts by a **Chartered Accountant**, who files an audit report (Form 3CA or 3CB with Form 3CD) with the Income Tax Department. It is required when your business or profession is above certain limits.

## When is it required?
| Case | Audit required if... |
|---|---|
| **Business** | turnover is more than **₹1 crore** |
| **Business with little cash** (cash receipts ≤ 5% of total receipts **and** cash payments ≤ 5% of total payments) | turnover is more than **₹10 crore** |
| **Profession** | gross receipts are more than **₹50 lakh** |
| **Presumptive scheme users** | you declare **less** than the presumptive income (e.g. below 8%/6% under 44AD) **and** your total income is above the basic exemption limit, in the situations the law specifies |

## Due dates (for FY 2025-26)
- **Audit report:** 30 September 2026
- **Income tax return (audit cases):** 31 October 2026

## Other audits change the ITR due date too
Companies must have their accounts audited under company law, and LLPs above certain limits under LLP law. When your accounts must be audited under **any** law, the ITR due date is generally **31 October**, even if a tax audit under section 44AB does not apply. Check the limits that apply to your entity.

## Penalty for not getting audited
A penalty can apply for failing to get the audit done or file the report on time. A CA can advise on the exact exposure.

## Under the new Income-tax Act, 2025
From tax year 2026-27, the tax audit provision is **section 63**. The limits are carried forward.
