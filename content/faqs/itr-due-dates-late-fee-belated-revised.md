# ITR due dates, late fee, and belated, revised and updated returns

## Due dates for income of FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)
| Who | Due date |
|---|---|
| Individuals and HUFs **without business income** (usually ITR-1 and ITR-2) | **31 July 2026** |
| Individuals and HUFs **with business or professional income, no audit** (usually ITR-3 and ITR-4) | **31 August 2026** |
| Anyone whose accounts **must be audited** (tax audit cases, companies, firms and LLPs under audit) | **31 October 2026** |
| Cases needing a transfer-pricing report | **30 November 2026** |

The **31 August** date for non-audit business and professional cases is new, from the Finance Act, 2026; earlier it was 31 July. For **firms filing ITR-5 without audit**, check the due date shown on the e-filing portal. The CBDT can also extend due dates by notification, so always check the portal.

## If you miss the due date: belated return
- You can still file a **belated return until 31 December 2026**.
- **Late fee (section 234F):** ₹5,000, or **₹1,000 if total income does not exceed ₹5 lakh**. No fee if your income is below the amount on which filing is compulsory.
- **Interest (section 234A):** 1% per month on unpaid tax, from the day after the due date.
- Most **business losses cannot be carried forward** from a belated return.

## Correcting a filed return: revised return
- You can file a **revised return until 31 March 2027** for AY 2026-27. The window was extended by the Finance Act, 2026; earlier it ended on 31 December.
- A revision filed **after 31 December 2026** attracts a fee. Revising early avoids it.

## Updated return (ITR-U)
- An **updated return** lets you report income you missed, **up to 48 months** after the end of the assessment year.
- You pay the tax due **plus additional tax**, which increases the longer you wait.
- It cannot be used to reduce income or claim a bigger refund.

## Under the new Act
For tax year 2026-27 onwards, the return-filing rules are in section 263 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. The late-fee amounts are reported to be carried forward unchanged; confirm on the portal when filing for that year.

## In CA Helper
Your ITR due date depends on your entity type and on whether an audit applies. Both come from your business profile.
