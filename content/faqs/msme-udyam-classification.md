# MSME classification and Udyam registration

## How is a business classified?
Since **1 April 2025**, a business is classified by **two tests together**: investment in plant, machinery or equipment, **and** annual turnover.

| Category | Investment up to | Turnover up to |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | ₹2.5 crore | ₹10 crore |
| **Small** | ₹25 crore | ₹100 crore |
| **Medium** | ₹125 crore | ₹500 crore |

A business must satisfy **both** limits for a category. If it exceeds either one, it moves to the next category. The same limits apply to manufacturing and service businesses.

**Example:** a service business with ₹50 lakh of equipment and ₹20 crore turnover is **Small**. It is within the Micro investment limit, but its turnover is above the Micro limit.

The earlier limits (Micro: ₹1 crore / ₹5 crore) no longer apply.

## Udyam registration
- **Udyam** is the official MSME registration. It is **free** and done online on the Udyam Registration portal.
- You register using **Aadhaar** (of the proprietor, a partner or a director) and the business **PAN**.
- Investment and turnover figures are taken largely from your **income tax and GST records**, so the category can be updated automatically.
- Registration is voluntary, but many government schemes, priority lending and protections for delayed payments from buyers require it.

## In CA Helper
Your profile shows your MSME tier based on the investment and turnover you entered. It is a guide; your official category is the one on your Udyam certificate.
