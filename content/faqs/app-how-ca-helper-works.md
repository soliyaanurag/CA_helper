# How CA Helper works, and when to consult a CA

## What CA Helper does
- **Works out which filings apply to you** (ITR, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, CMP-08, GSTR-4, TDS returns) from your business profile, and when each is due.
- **Reminds you** before each due date and when something is overdue.
- **Guides self-filing** with explanations, checklists and step-by-step instructions for each form.
- **Connects you with a CA** at a listed price, or pro-bono for eligible small businesses.
- **Keeps proof**: your documents and filing acknowledgements, stored encrypted.

## What CA Helper does *not* do
- It **does not file returns for you**. Filing happens on the official portals (GST portal, income tax e-filing, TRACES), by you or your CA.
- Its answers are **general information, not legal or tax advice** for your specific situation.
- Legal limits and due dates are being verified; the official portal is always the final word.

## What the statuses mean
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Upcoming | Due later; nothing to do yet |
| Docs pending | The checklist has items you still need to gather |
| Ready | The checklist is complete; you can file |
| With CA | A CA you engaged is handling this filing |
| Filed | Marked as filed |
| Filed–verified | Filed, and the acknowledgement has been checked |
| Overdue | The due date has passed and it isn't filed yet |

## Who can see your data
- **Your CA** can see only the filings you gave them, and only the documents linked to those filings, while the engagement is active.
- **Admins** can see account information but never your document contents.
- PAN, GSTIN, TAN, phone number and uploaded files are **stored encrypted**.

## When should you consult a CA?
- You receive a **notice** from the Income Tax Department or GST department.
- Your business may need a **tax audit**, or its accounts must be audited under company or LLP law.
- You're **crossing a limit**: GST registration, leaving the composition scheme, or the presumptive scheme limits.
- You need to file a **belated, revised or updated** return with tax payable, or you've missed several returns.
- You're unsure which **ITR form** or **GST scheme** fits you.
- **Companies and LLPs** also have ROC/MCA filings, which CA Helper does not track.
